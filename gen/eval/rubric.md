# Generator draft review rubric (U15)

For Anantha. One pass per draft in `evidence/U15/drafts/`. A draft ships
only after it validates clean AND passes all three rows below.

## Kind

- No line states the answer before the child gives it.
- No line marks a wrong answer wrong: no wrong, incorrect, try again,
  no red-X language of any kind.
- The mascot tone is patient and curious, never sad or cross.

## Socratic

- Exactly one question per bubble, one action asked for at a time.
- Hints climb: a smaller question, then a visual cue, then a worked
  mini-story with different numbers, then the original question again.
- The worked example could not be mistaken for this problem.

## On goal

- The story, questions, and reactions match the spec id and standard.
- Numbers on screen can only come from the problem: start, step sizes,
  solver totals. Nothing else looks like a count.
- Verdict per draft: approve, or the one-line fix a human must make.
